Enough Talk

Short-form video work created for Enough Talk.

Company overview

Enough Talk on Instagram is a Texas-based platform that promotes local businesses and artists. Its work gives the people and places shaping Texas a stronger local spotlight.

In addition to editing video for Enough Talk, I operate the camera during shoots and assist with lighting, sound, and creative decisions. That production support helps each piece move smoothly from an idea on set to a finished short-form story.
